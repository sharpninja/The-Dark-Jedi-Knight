# II. Sensitivity

For weeks Bruce has been on Mustafar in Vader's fortress learning about the Force.  He is not Vader's first Padawan, but definitely the most powerful. 

Over time, Bruce learns how Vader used the Light to find someone capable of stopping Darth Sidius.  Once he found Bruce, the Dark showed him where a converge between galaxies would help him reach Earth.  It required breaking through hyperspace lanes at full speed, so Vader had the power of his Command Ship doubled.  Even still, Vader had to use the force to guide them through the tiny window for them to breach the correct hyperspace lane in our galaxy.

Once at Earth, Vader found Bruce our hunting gangsters doing the Joker's bidding.  Bruce remembers taking down a group and then being chocked by something he could fight, then waking up in Vader's brig.

But these last few weeks with Vader have been incredible.  Already the master of all of Earth's martial arts, Bruce has been learning new techniques of meditation and focusing his will and rage to increase his effectiveness in combat.  Bruce can feel the force in everything and everyone and is learning to converse with non force users telepathically, no matter the language they speak.  Bruce even has begun to see the memories of others.

Vader comes to Bruce's quarters carrying light sabre.  "Do you know what this is?"

"A weapon of great power."

"In the hands of a Sith Lord or Jedi Knight, this weapon surpasses all others.  Technically it is just focused plasma.  It takes a great deal of power to make that plasma, which we use Kiber Crystals for.  It takes much more power to control the weapon, to make it part of you.  Younglings spend years learning how to walk and run with it without hurting themselves.  We don't have that much time.

"You are already an accomplished swordsman, so I believe you will handle a light sabre with the appropriate respect."  Vader hands Bruce the sabre.  Bruce holds it out in front of him, studying its design.  He notes the center of gravity and the feel of the activation slider under his thumb.

"This is amazing workmanship.  Who makes these?" asks Bruce.

"Each Sith or Jedi builds their own as part of their training.  I was careless and had to build several.  I think I got good at it.  The one you are holding was the last that I made as a Jedi.  This one," Vader activates his red sabre, "this one will be my last. My limbs are all prosthetic now, a circumstance of fighting my old Jedi master before I could control the Dark Side of the Force.  Light sabre duels end when some loses enough limbs or life such they cannot continue.  I have no limbs left to give."

Vader shows Bruce some basic stances and proper handling of an active sabre.  Soon Bruce is adapting Japanese forms to the reality of the Light Sabre and soon feels natural using it instead of a sword.  The light weight greatly reduces his fatigue while armored leading Vader to suggest he never take it off unless he is securely alone.  Bruce is assigned an R2 unit and he is amazed to find he completely understand the droid and that the droid responds to English despite Bruce not hearing it spoke here.

Bruce and the droid develop battle tactics after he gives the R2 unit a makeover which includes new paint including a subtle grey bat logo.  The R2 unit also teaches Bruce about Emperial technology and troop rotations.  Finally, Bruce learns about Emperor Palpatines career as a Sith, Politician and Warlord.
