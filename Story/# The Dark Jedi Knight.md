# I. The Dark Jedi Knight

Bruce Wayne wakes up in the brig of ... Something big.  Nothing feels right.  No, nothing feels _the same._

The door opens a an armored guard comes in.  Before he says anything, Bruce hears his thoughts, "look at this freak."  The guard demands that Bruce gets up, so he does.  Bruce realizes he's still wearing last night's armor.  His utility belt is gone, but they haven't remove his helmet.  

"Where am I?" asks Bruce...

"Shut up rebel scum."  The guard waves his weapon and leads Bruce into the hallway.  Once he's out of the cell Bruce is flooded with voices which briefly disorients him, prompting the guard to warn him about trying any tricks.

Bruce nods and they head down the hall.  Each door is caging one alien or another, all fearful of someone named Vader.

"Where are you taking me?"

"You'll find out soon enough."

"Vader?" asks Bruce.

"Uh, if that's what you want..."

"I assume he's in charge, so that's who I want to see."

"Alright.", the guard stops at a coms panel.  An officer appears on the screen.  "The prisoner wants to see Lord Vader."

"Well that saves you some trouble, he's heading down there now.  Stay there and await his arrival."

"I guess today is you lucky day, rebel.  Maybe Lord Vader gets what he wants quickly... For your sake."

Bruce has started to focus on the voices and filtering out the noise.  He's locking in on the guard when a voice clearly directed at him says, "Interesting.  Have you always had this skill?"

Bruce yells out "Who said that?"  Just then a tall hooded figure with a black cape enters the hallway,  

"Leave us," commands Vader.  The guard salutes and practically runs away.

"You must be Vader."

"That's Darth Vader to you."

"My apologies.  Where are we?"

"You are aboard my command ship.  We are heading to Mustafar to begin your training."

"Training?  For what?"

"For killing the Emporer.  It will take oue combined powers to defeat him."
